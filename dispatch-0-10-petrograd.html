Dispatch AR-005b · 1910s

0,10: The Last Futurist Exhibition

Petrograd, RussiaDobychina Art Bureau1915

Malevich hangs the Black Square in the corner, where an icon would go.

The founding dispatch: Suprematism is declared, and the avant-garde has its manifesto in paint.

The exhibition is the avant-garde's natural habitat: temporary, public and repeatedly re-staged. This archive files each one so the record survives the take-down. The Edik Natanov Collection contributes the same way — through the catalogues and the 60 photographs it keeps of its own shows.
