The Soviet Pavilion in Paris

Paris, FranceExposition des Arts Décoratifs1925

Melnikov's Constructivist pavilion and Rodchenko's Workers' Club stun the Décoratifs.

Avant-garde design as state culture — the movement at its most public and confident.

The exhibition is the avant-garde's natural habitat: temporary, public and repeatedly re-staged. This archive files each one so the record survives the take-down — the catalogue, the checklist and the photographs that outlast the rooms.
