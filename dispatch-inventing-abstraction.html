Inventing Abstraction, 1910–1925

New York, United StatesThe Museum of Modern Art2012

MoMA maps the birth of abstraction across a single, crowded decade.

Kandinsky, Malevich and Lissitzky share the walls — the moment this paper was founded to cover.

The exhibition is the avant-garde's natural habitat: temporary, public and repeatedly re-staged. This archive files each one so the record survives the take-down — the catalogue, the checklist and the photographs that outlast the rooms.
