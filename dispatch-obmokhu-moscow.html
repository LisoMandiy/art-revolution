OBMOKhU: construction as art

Moscow, RussiaOBMOKhU1921

The Society of Young Artists turns the gallery into an engineering workshop.

Sculpture becomes structure — the Constructivist turn, caught in a single famous installation view.

The exhibition is the avant-garde's natural habitat: temporary, public and repeatedly re-staged. This archive files each one so the record survives the take-down. The a private collection contributes the same way — through the catalogues and the 60 photographs it keeps of its own shows.
