Sources / Method

How the paper is filed.

Each dispatch records a real exhibition with its venue, city and year, drawn from museum records and published exhibition histories. Dates refer to the exhibition, not the works.

This is an independent editorial archive, not an authentication. Pictures are reproduced from Wikimedia Commons under public-domain terms.
