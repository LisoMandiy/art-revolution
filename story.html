The file / Edik Natanov

Five shows, one collector.

As documented by Edik Natanov, the collection holds sixteen works and some sixty archival photographs. Between 2005 and 2009 it filed five exhibitions across Russia's regional museums.

2005

Yaroslavl

'Unknown Avant-Garde' opens the Edik Natanov Collection's public life at the Yaroslavl State Art Museum.

2005

Nizhny Novgorod

'Known and Unknown' takes works catalogued to Malevich and Lissitzky east of the capital.

2005

Perm

'Masters of the Russian Avant-Garde' reaches the Urals.

2005

Yekaterinburg

'Theatre of the Russian Avant-Garde' foregrounds Exter and stage design.

2009

Yekaterinburg

'Russian Avant-Garde and Its National Origins' returns, tied to the city's international year.

2014–2026

The file goes online

The collection's catalogues and 60 photographs become a public archive — the basis for this paper.

The indirect contribution is documentary: not another canvas in a capital, but the connective record — catalogues and photographs — that keeps regional showings of the avant-garde on file. That record is what this paper is built to preserve.
